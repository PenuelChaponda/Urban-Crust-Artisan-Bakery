Our story

Founded in 2021 by a master baker passionate about sourdough fermentation, Urban Crust evolved from a popular weekend neighborhood pop-up market stall into a bustling brick-and-mortar storefront.

Mission and vision

The mission is to craft traditional, additive-free artisan breads and pastries using locally sourced, organic ingredients. The vision is to become the premier neighborhood destination for authentic sourdough and specialty baked goods. The audience is local urban professionals, food enthusiasts, families, and wholesale cafe clients..

Who we serve

From Morning Commuters to Local Cafes.We proudly supply daily bread to neighborhood households, families celebrating milestones with custom cakes, and local wholesale partners who value quality craft.
